## zh-Hant

- 新增「稍後提醒我」：Claude 等你回覆時，可以在輸入框上方選 15 分鐘、30 分鐘或 1 小時後再提醒
- 修正 Claude 等你回覆超過 30 分鐘後，桌面貓會顯示睡覺的問題
- 修正 Claude 沒有在等你回覆時，桌面貓卻伸手的問題

## en

- New "Remind me later": when Claude is waiting for your reply, choose 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1 hour above the message box, and the cat calls you again then
- Fixes an issue where Desktop Cat appeared asleep after Claude had waited more than 30 minutes for your reply
- Fixes an issue where Desktop Cat called for you when Claude wasn't waiting for your reply
